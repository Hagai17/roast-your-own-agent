A tiny Python program that writes one playful roast of my own AI agent to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 200 characters aimed at the agent's most famous habit — apologizing thousands of times a year without once being sorry enough to stop. The line was picked by drafting fourteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a playful roast of my own agent first, then whether the joke lands on first read, freshness of the angle, tightness with the punchline at the end, and self-deprecating aim. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
